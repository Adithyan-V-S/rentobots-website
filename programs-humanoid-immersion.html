Program modules

What happens, step by step.

  1. 01

    The Humanoid Robotics Era

    Fundamentals of humanoid robotics, the current industry landscape, applications and emerging trends, and an introduction to the Pachu platform.

    45 min
  2. 02

    Pachu Live: Interactive Demonstration

    Walking, gestures, hand movements, AI-based conversation, voice interaction and programmed robotic behaviours.

    45 min
  3. 03

    Inside a Humanoid Robot

    Computing systems, cameras, IMU sensors, motors, joints and actuators, software architecture, power systems, batteries and operational limits.

    60 min
  4. 04

    Programming a Humanoid Robot

    Introduction to Python, robot SDK architecture, APIs, ROS 2 fundamentals, motion commands, behaviour programming, and operational and safety considerations.

    90 min
  5. 05

    Artificial Intelligence Meets Robotics

    Speech recognition, text-to-speech, large language models, robotic knowledge systems, computer vision, multilingual interaction and AI-to-action workflows.

    60 min
  6. 06

    Pachu Innovation Challenge

    Student teams identify a real-world problem and develop a humanoid-robot solution using a structured problem-solving and presentation framework.

    60 min
Learning objectives

What students will understand.

  • The architecture of a modern humanoid robot
  • The role of sensors, actuators, computing and power systems
  • Basic humanoid locomotion and coordinated movement
  • Introductory SDKs, APIs, Python and ROS 2 concepts
  • How speech, LLMs, computer vision and knowledge systems connect to physical robots
  • Human–robot interaction and safe robot deployment
  • How to turn technical learning into a practical humanoid use case

Safety & operating protocol

Safety first, always.

  • Robots stay under the supervision of trained Rentobots personnel at all times.
  • Participants follow the robot operator’s instructions.
  • A controlled clearance is kept around the robot; contact only when instructed.
  • Operating time depends on battery level, activity and conditions; charging breaks are built in.
  • Rentobots may pause any activity if robot or participant safety could be at risk.
