FAQ

Before you invest.

Do I need robotics experience?+

No. Rentobots supplies trained operators, handles every booking and maintains the robots. You don’t need to operate anything yourself.

What exactly is the 80 km territory?+

As a City Partner you get exclusive Rentobots rights within an 80 km radius of your chosen city. Bookings inside that area go to your robots first.

What does a State Partner get?+

Exclusive Rentobots rights across the whole state, a larger fleet, a dedicated operator team and priority on state-level corporate and institutional deals.

How do I earn?+

You receive an agreed share of the revenue from bookings your robots complete, with monthly statements and payouts. The exact share is set in your partner agreement.

Are earnings guaranteed?+

No. Earnings depend on bookings in your territory. We share local demand data and a business plan with you before you invest.

Who owns the robots?+

You do. Ownership, operations and responsibilities are set out clearly in the partner agreement.

Can I move from City to State Partner?+

Yes, if the state territory is available. City Partners get the first opportunity to upgrade in their state.
