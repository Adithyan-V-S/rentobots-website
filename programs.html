Program FAQ

Good to know.

Do students operate the robot themselves?+

Pachu always stays under the supervision of trained Rentobots operators. Students program and interact with the robot only in supervised exercises. The Certified Robot Operator workshop is where participants learn to operate robots hands-on.

Which program should our college start with?+

Most institutions start with the 1-day Humanoid Robotics Immersion. If you want a campus-wide launch moment first, begin with the 3-hour Campus Experience. We can recommend the right fit after a short preview with your faculty.

Do participants get a certificate?+

Yes. The Immersion, Developer Workshop and Semester Program include participation certificates. The operator workshop ends in a written and practical assessment for the Certified Robot Operator credential.

Where do you run programs?+

We are based in Kozhikode and run programs across Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Bangalore and Hyderabad, and elsewhere in India on request. Travel and robot logistics outside our operating region are quoted separately.

What does the institution need to provide?+

An indoor venue with a controlled demonstration area, reliable power, Wi-Fi, a projector or LED screen, an audio system, tables for technical sessions, and a faculty coordinator.

How is pricing decided?+

Each proposal is tailored to the program, participant count, location and customisation. Message us and we will send a proposal for your institution.
