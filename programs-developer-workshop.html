Humanoid Developer Workshop

Three days. One working prototype.

For students who want to build, not just watch. Three days of deeper technical work on humanoid architecture, robot software and AI integration, with hands-on time on the humanoid and the robot dog, ending in a team prototype.

Program modules

What happens, step by step.

  1. Day 1

    Humanoid Architecture & Operation

    G1 hardware overview, degrees of freedom, sensors, actuators, safety, operation modes and a practical demonstration.

  2. Day 2

    SDK, Programming & Motion Development

    Development environment, SDK and API concepts, robot communication, motion commands, data access and the programming workflow.

  3. Day 3

    AI, Research & Prototype Lab

    Perception and AI integration, the reinforcement-learning workflow, research directions, then team prototype build and review.

Hands-on

Two robots, real hardware.

Humanoid · Pachu

Quadruped · Kuttappan

Team prototype

Each team applies what it learned to a working prototype and presents it on the final day.

Safety & operating protocol

Safety first, always.

  • Robots stay under the supervision of trained Rentobots personnel at all times.
  • Participants follow the robot operator’s instructions.
  • A controlled clearance is kept around the robot; contact only when instructed.
  • Operating time depends on battery level, activity and conditions; charging breaks are built in.
  • Rentobots may pause any activity if robot or participant safety could be at risk.
