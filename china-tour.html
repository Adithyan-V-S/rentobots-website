FAQ

Good to know.

Who leads the tour?+

An experienced Rentobots tour lead travels with the group the whole way, with an interpreter for company visits and business meetings.

Do I need to speak Chinese?+

No. Interpretation is included for visits and meetings, and our team handles all local arrangements.

Is the visa included?+

We help you with the China visa process and documents. Our team will confirm visa requirements for your nationality when you enquire.

How big is the group?+

A maximum of 15 people per batch, so every workshop and workspace visit stays hands-on.

Can I join on my own?+

Yes. Most travellers join individually and meet business-minded people from across the industry.

What are 6S and 7S stores?+

Robot experience stores that bring sales, service, spare parts and live demos together in one place — a model you can take home to your own market.

Will I be able to buy robots or sign deals?+

Yes. At the supply-chain mini fair you can discuss purchases, distribution and partnerships directly with suppliers. Purchases are not included in the tour price.

What about food?+

Daily meals are included, and vegetarian options are available throughout the tour.
